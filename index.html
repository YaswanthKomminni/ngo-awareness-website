About InAmigos

Empowering Lives & Transforming Communities

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020, by Govind Shukla. The organization is committed to creating positive social change through various community-driven initiatives.

The foundation works tirelessly across multiple sectors including quality education, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, animal welfare, skill development, and community welfare. Supported by thousands of volunteers, interns, and partners, InAmigos Foundation has positively impacted thousands of lives across India.

Our Philosophy

Why Our Work Matters

Millions of people still struggle with poverty, lack of quality education, structural unemployment, and severe environmental challenges. Through targeted, community-driven projects focused on education, female financial independence, ecological sustainability, and street animal welfare, InAmigos Foundation actively works to bridge these gaps.

Bridging Opportunity Gaps

Establishing digital classrooms and support structures in rural schools to give equal growth opportunities to all children.

Sustainable Empowerment

Moving away from temporary relief towards teaching lifelong skills, fostering business and financial independence.

Building Compassionate Ecosystems

Ensuring our local stray animals are fed, sheltered, and medically treated to nurture empathy across human communities.

Every volunteer, donor, and supporter contributes towards building a stronger and more inclusive society. Through small, sustained collective actions, we hold the key to creating massive generational shifts.

Govind Shukla

Founder, InAmigos Foundation
